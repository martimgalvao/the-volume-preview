Content on the walls

One strip around the room, NE · SE · SW · NW, 12000 × 1144 px (8 px/in), from the top of the baseboard to the underside of the grid pipe. Anything you load is treated as that strip and projected by the six projectors, so it lands on door frames, speakers and the mirror as it would in the room. Build to the template.

Screen share: pick the window that shows your strip (Resolume output, a player, a browser tab). Camera: a virtual camera fed by Syphon/Spout/NDI works the same way. NDI (desktop app only): receive a Resolume NDI output or any other NDI sender directly, on this machine or over the network. Definition and template: data/projection_strip.json, projection_strip.png.
